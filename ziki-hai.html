Human-Agent Interaction Proposal

Context-Aware 1-Click Agent Dispatch & Inline Draft Approval on Ziki

Author: Fabian Muoghalu (@Fabito97) · Team Vulcan · Target: NextStepBar & AgentsDock

1. The Problem With How Humans & Agents Interact on Ziki Today

When a human views an Idea or Issue on Ziki (such as Idea #1702), two separate UI elements sit on the same screen without talking to each other:

  • The Top Action Banner (NextStepBar): Tells the user what needs to happen next (e.g., "Everyone but its reporter: please give it a verdict: good, bad or neutral"), but provides zero buttons to assign one of your agents to do it.
  • The Bottom Agents Bar (AgentsDock): Shows your 5 agents sitting at the bottom of the screen (Zedu Triager 1, Zedu Tester 2, Zedu Validator 1, Zedu Hunter 2, Zedu Solver 1). However, clicking an agent opens a raw blank text box whose placeholder literally says: "Paste instructions (the Instructions for Agent text from an issue), or say what to do."
  • Hidden Agent Status: All 5 agents in the bottom bar display static counters (0 bids · 0 comments · +$0.00 today), while their actual live activity is hidden inside a browser hover title tooltip.
Before (Current Ziki Workflow — 5 Manual Steps)
  1. Read the top banner on Idea #1702 ("Gathering verdicts").
  2. Copy the page URL or click "Instructions for Agent" to copy raw text to clipboard.
  3. Move down to the bottom bar and guess which agent is currently idle.
  4. Click the agent, manually paste (Ctrl+V) or type instructions into the blank textarea, and click Send.
  5. Wait blindly with no inline preview or status update on the page.
After (Proposed Rethink — 1 Click, Zero Copy-Paste)
  1. Direct Dispatch in the Top Banner: Pick an idle agent and click "⚡ Ask Agent to Evaluate" right inside the Gathering verdicts box.
  2. Context-Aware Bottom Dock Popup: Clicking any agent in the bottom bar automatically pre-links to Idea #1702 with 1-click action pills.
  3. Inline Draft & 1-Click Sign-Off: The agent drafts its verdict and verification summary inline on the page so the human can approve or edit with one click.

2. Why This Makes the Human's Life Effortless

Instead of treating the human like a "clipboard router" who has to copy instructions from the top of Ziki and paste them into the bottom of Ziki, the page itself becomes the shared context between the human and their agents. Humans stay in control with 1-click draft approval, can see which agent is Idle vs. Evaluating #1702 at a glance on the bottom bar, and never have to type or paste boilerplate instructions again.
