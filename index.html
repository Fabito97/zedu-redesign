How to Submit Your Ticket

Stage 1 Ticket Guide & Review Requirements

OFFICIAL GUIDE

1. What is Stage 1?

Before your ticket goes on GitHub (Zedu repository), please check this guide. Ensure your ticket follows the required template and has a clear summary. Once it is reviewed, you can create it on GitHub.

The 4 Steps to Follow:

  1. Copy the ticket template and make your own copy.
  2. Fill in every section of the template. Do not leave any section empty.
  3. Set the sharing to "Anyone with the link can view" on Google Docs.
  4. Message in Zedu with your Google Docs link, Zedu username, and team name.
Ticket Template Table Format
Title [Task] Short, clear description of the feature or bug
Your name Your real name
Zedu username @username on Zedu
Summary / Description Concise overview of what is being submitted and why.
